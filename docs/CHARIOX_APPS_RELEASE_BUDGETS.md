Numbers apply to both machines unless a platform is specified. CPU units are cores, so 1 core means 100% of one CPU, not 100% of the host. MiB/GiB are binary. Use nearest-rank p95, `sorted[ceil(0.95*n)-1]`, and include sample count, maximum, failures and censored observations. Small samples below 20 are provisional. A timed-out or missing observation cannot be counted as a successful p95 sample.

Measure four admitted workers, first with no views, then with four App views and slow broker consumers. Count worker RSS/physical footprint including Buffer/native/WASM allocations and threads, trusted broker allocations, and App-induced browser renderer/worker/GPU/cache memory. Attribute shared browser costs conservatively through baseline subtraction and report the full browser total too. Kernel/provider baseline is separate; preserve its reserve. Linux cgroups and the Mac worker monitor do not include trusted broker or browser processes.

## Release interpretation

This proposal removes the missing-number blocker, not missing measurements or existing failures. The sweep's historical V1-INT-22 label refers to startup/load budgets; newer plan revisions put lifecycle/throughput in V1-INT-21 and binding policy in V1-INT-22. Keep both row meaning and plan revision explicit in ledger updates.

Initial evidence before this proposal: cold start and kernel query targets pass; the single-attempt storm does not validate retries, and the censored first inbox observation prevents all-event p95 acceptance. Post-proposal candidate-g rerun, `takeover/sweep-g/pkg/budgets/timings.json`: five cold starts p95 4.815 s pass; five eventual storm starts including busy retries max 20.843 s pass the 90 s target, accepted-to-running p95 11.381 s passes 20 s; warm inbox n=10 p95 35.007 ms passes, acceptance p95 11.634 ms passes. Kernel n=553 p95 7.677 ms passes but max 161.328 ms fails 100 ms. No target was widened after this run. V1-INT-03 worker growth/recovery subsets pass the 3 s tool-to-stop target, while peak overshoot, aggregate/browser resource accounting and the original signed slow-consumer matrix remain partial. V-RUN-05 measured tool/lifecycle/due-wake subsets pass the numeric timing targets. T-08 remains failed on correctness; V1-INT-15 remains partial for input-to-painted-frame timing and the excluded restart/IME matrix. V-RUN-01/02 can reuse kernel/neighbour targets but require matching CPU/peak traces before full resource-budget acceptance. G-Isolation and G-Evidence inherit those open measurements.
